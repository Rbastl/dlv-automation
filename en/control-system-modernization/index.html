Control System Modernization

We modernize automation hardware and software while preserving process behavior and existing equipment where it still makes sense to keep it.

When this is useful

Modernization becomes necessary when PLCs or HMIs are obsolete, spare parts are unavailable, source projects are difficult to maintain, new equipment must be integrated or the current platform limits further development.

What we do

Assessment of current hardware and software architecture.
Migration plan for PLC, HMI, SCADA and network interfaces.
Transfer and verification of existing algorithms.
New diagnostics, signals and integration functions.
Staged commissioning, backups and rollback planning.

How we work

For operating production lines, the key objective is not just hardware replacement but predictable behavior after migration. Critical modes and test scenarios are agreed before the cutover.

What you receive

Modernized control system.
Source projects for the new software.
Description of migrated and modified functions.
Backups and a defined maintenance baseline.
