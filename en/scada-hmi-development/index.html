SCADA and HMI Development

We build operator interfaces that make equipment status, stop reasons and available actions clear. Visualization is designed around the process and operating workflow rather than around a raw tag list.

When this is useful

Typical projects include new lines, expansion of an existing SCADA, replacement of obsolete HMIs, a common supervisory interface for multiple machines or visualization on top of existing PLC logic.

What we do

Process overview screens and equipment faceplates.
Alarms, warnings, events and acknowledgement logic.
Trends, archives and process-data history.
Recipes, setpoints, access levels and maintenance functions.
Interfaces with PLCs, databases, OPC UA and adjacent systems.

How we work

We define screen hierarchy, navigation, alarm philosophy, color rules and archived data before implementation. This keeps the interface useful during real production faults.

What you receive

HMI/SCADA source project.
Configured screens, alarms and diagnostics.
Trends, archives and reporting functions within scope.
Backups and material for future maintenance.
